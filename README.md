# Demos

Public video demos by Andrii Yevenko, served with GitHub Pages at https://ytsuhen.github.io/Pub-demos/.

- [`first-visit/`](first-visit/): a business-analysis demo for homecare software. One caregiver's first visit, shown twice (texts and paper vs an app), then the visit's state machine, transition matrix, requirements traced to their sources, and a pre-fill trade-off. The agency, people and app are fictional. Three versions: no voice, a synthetic narrator, and the author's voice cloned from his recordings.

Who did what: the analysis, the requirements and the review are mine; the pages and the videos are built by AI coding agents (Claude Code) from my prompts.

The index page also links to two demos that live in their own repositories, each with its own line on who did what:

- [Clinical rules in CQL over FHIR](https://ytsuhen.github.io/CQL-Demo-Yevenko/) ([repository](https://github.com/ytsuhen/CQL-Demo-Yevenko)): headache triage and PHQ-9 / GAD-7 screening as executable clinical rules with tests.
- [MedReg Navigator](https://ytsuhen.github.io/MedRegNav/) ([repository](https://github.com/ytsuhen/MedRegNav)): a proof of concept that maps EU rules for medical software. In Ukrainian with an English switch.
